Family Notes

Reminder

Puerto Rico is a US territory — no passport required for US citizens, but everyone needs a valid government-issued photo ID. Check expiration dates now!

Reminder

Jordan: your return is July 8 on UA1173 (SJU 1:25 PM). You'll need to arrange a separate transfer to the airport — you are NOT on the group shuttle (that's July 9).

Kids

The snorkel tour on July 6 departs at 7:30 AM — get kids to bed early on July 5! Motion sickness medication is strongly recommended for the boat ride to Vieques.

Health

Pack reef-safe sunscreen SPF 50+, aloe vera, bug repellent, and all prescription meds for the full trip. Two booster seats are included in both shuttle bookings.

Food

Resort restaurants fill up fast in July. Call (787) 863-1000 to reserve group dinners — especially the farewell dinner on July 8. Lunch on July 5 is on your own.
